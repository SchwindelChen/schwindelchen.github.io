About me

About

Zhaoyu Chen is a Ph.D. student in Political Economy at the School of Economics, Peking University, where he is supervised by Professor Min Fang.

He graduated from the Hongyi Honor College at Wuhan University, where he majored in Politics, Economics, and Philosophy (PPE) and received a Bachelor of Laws degree. As an undergraduate, he was supervised by Professor Zhixuan Feng and led a Luojia Undergraduate Innovation Research Fund project entitled “Research on a Hierarchical Measurement System for Total Labor Productivity.”

Research interests

Research interests

My main research areas are the Political Economy of Development, Computational Methods in Political Economy, and Socialist Political Economy with Chinese Characteristics.

Recent interests include:

  • State–capital relations in the world-system and the development of Global South economies
  • Technological progress, capital accumulation, and economic growth in China
  • Political Economy of Innovation Systems
  • Applications of computational methods—including agent-based modeling (ABM), dynamical systems, and statistical equilibrium—to political economy
