1. 批判实在论、地方性知识与经济学自主知识体系——一个基于 ABM 的考察Critical Realism, Local Knowledge, and Autonomous Knowledge Systems in Economics: An Agent-based Modeling Investigation

冯志轩*、陈昭宇Zhixuan Feng* and Zhaoyu Chen

《政治经济学季刊》2025 年第 2 期。Political Economy Quarterly, No. 2, 2025 (in Chinese).

摘要与关键词Abstract and keywords

本文尝试以相对形式化的方法，探讨经济学自主知识体系相较于其他知识体系的潜在优势。基于批判实在论、社会定位理论与地方性知识视角，本文构建了一个包含多层次行为逻辑的基于主体建模（Agent-based Model, ABM）框架，并借助该模型分析了在不同社会本体论设定下，自主知识体系与普遍性知识体系等在解释及预测经济活动方面的能力差异。研究发现，若经济系统中存在影响主体行为的宏观结构、社会关系或个体间网络化复杂互动中的任一情形，自主知识体系均能展现出更优的解释力与预测力。这一结论为构建经济学自主知识体系提供了具有理论一般性的支撑。This paper aims to adopt a relatively formalized approach to explore the potential advantages of autonomous knowledge systems within the field of economics compared to alternative epistemological frameworks. Drawing upon theoretical perspectives from Critical Realism, Social Positioning Theory, and Local Knowledge Paradigms, we construct a multi-layered behavioral logic embedded in an Agent-based Model. Employing this computational model, we systematically analyze the relationship between autonomous knowledge systems and other epistemological approaches—including universalist knowledge systems—in terms of their respective capacities for economic explanation and prediction under varying social ontological assumptions. The study finds that when economic systems manifest any of the following conditions: (1) macro-structural influences on agent behavior, (2) socially embedded relational configurations, or (3) complex networked interactions among individual actors, autonomous knowledge systems consistently exhibit superior explanatory and predictive power. These findings provide theoretically generalizable support for the systematic construction of autonomous knowledge frameworks in economic research.

关键词：Keywords: 自主知识体系；Agent-based Model；批判实在论；社会定位理论；地方性知识autonomous knowledge systems; Agent-based model; critical realism; social positioning theory; local knowledge paradigm

2. 政治经济学的竞争理论与“内卷式”竞争The Theory of Competition in Political Economy and “Involutionary” Competition

冯志轩、陈昭宇Zhixuan Feng and Zhaoyu Chen*

《中国人民大学学报》，拟录用。Provisionally accepted by Journal of Renmin University of China (in Chinese).

摘要与关键词Abstract and keywords

“内卷式”竞争概念的提出深化了政治经济学对市场竞争的认识。为理解“内卷式”竞争，本文立足马克思主义竞争理论，借鉴演化经济学对企业行为的分析，构建由竞争的一般规定性、竞争环境和竞争主体组成的分析框架。本文认为，竞争具有促进积累与诱发过度积累的两面性，其具体结果取决于竞争、积累和利润之间能否形成良性循环。“内卷式”竞争的可能性广泛存在于现代市场经济之中，但其具体表现具有历史特殊性。当前我国部分领域出现的“内卷式”竞争，是经济发展进入新的阶段后，竞争环境变化与企业行为调整相对滞后共同作用所产生的局部竞争现象。治理“内卷式”竞争，需要继续发挥中国特色社会主义经济制度的优势，根据新的发展阶段调整相关机制，并推动企业知识和能力的持续更新。The concept of “involutionary” competition has deepened political economy’s understanding of market competition. To understand this phenomenon, this paper draws on Marxist competition theory and evolutionary economics’ analysis of firm behavior to develop an analytical framework comprising the general determinations of competition, the competitive environment, and competing actors. Competition both promotes accumulation and induces overaccumulation; its specific outcome depends on whether competition, accumulation, and profit form a virtuous circle. The possibility of “involutionary” competition exists widely in modern market economies, although its concrete manifestations are historically specific. The “involutionary” competition now observed in some sectors of China is a localized phenomenon arising from changes in the competitive environment and relatively slow adjustments in firm behavior as the economy enters a new stage of development. Addressing it requires continuing to draw on the strengths of China’s socialist economic system, adjusting relevant mechanisms to the new stage of development, and promoting the continual renewal of firms’ knowledge and capabilities.

关键词：Keywords: “内卷式”竞争；马克思主义竞争理论；资本积累；企业能力“Involutionary” competition; Marxist competition theory; Capital accumulation; Firm capabilities

3. 马克思—斯拉法框架下的价值决定与地区间劳动生产率差距测量Value Determination and Regional Labor Productivity Differences Measurement under Marx-Sraffa Framework

冯志轩、陈昭宇Zhixuan Feng and Zhaoyu Chen

载荣兆梓、李帮喜主编：《马克思主义的全要素生产率：模型与算法》。In Rong Zhaozhi and Li Bangxi (eds.), Total Factor Productivity in Marxism: Method and Algorithms (in Chinese).

摘要与关键词Abstract and keywords

本文在马克思主义政治经济学的框架内研究多技术条件下的价值决定过程，并在此基础上开发了一种度量地区间劳动生产率差距的方法。文章采用马克思—斯拉法框架下的多技术线性生产模型，并利用 2007—2017 年地区投入产出表数据，比较了两种不同的价值决定方法，并计算了中国各地区的劳动生产率差距。文章发现，在价值决定问题上：村田方法和 CFFFV 方法尽管在理论上存在差异，但是在实际使用过程中差距很小，得到的结论基本相同。中国的地区间人均收入的差异主要是由劳动生产率决定的，但是价值转移也对地区间的收入差距有一定的影响。可以根据地区劳动生产率和价值转移的差异将样本中的地区划分为四种类型，每种类型所面临的发展问题是不同的。This paper investigates the value determination process under multi-technology conditions within the framework of Marxist political economy, and develops a methodology for measuring inter-regional labor productivity disparities. Employing the Marx-Sraffa multi-technology linear production model with Chinese regional input-output table data from 2007 to 2017, the study compares two distinct value determination methods and calculates labor productivity differences across Chinese regions. Key findings include: Firstly, regarding value determination, although theoretical differences exist between the Murata method and CFFFV method, their practical applications demonstrate minimal divergence with fundamentally consistent conclusions. Secondly, China's inter-regional disparities in per capita income are primarily determined by labor productivity differentials, while value transfer also exerts a measurable influence on regional income gaps. Thirdly, Regional samples can be categorized into four typologies based on labor productivity and value transfer differentials, each facing distinct developmental challenges.

关键词：Keywords: 马克思—斯拉法框架；多技术线性生产模型；价值决定；区域间劳动生产率差距Marx-Sraffa Framework; Multi-technology Linear Production Model; the Determination of value; Regional Labor Productivity Differences
