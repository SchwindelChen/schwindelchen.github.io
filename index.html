About me

个人简介About

陈昭宇，北京大学经济学院 2026 级政治经济学方向博士研究生，师从方敏教授。Zhaoyu Chen is a Ph.D. student in Political Economy at the School of Economics, Peking University, where he is supervised by Professor Min Fang.

本科毕业于武汉大学弘毅学堂政治学、经济学与哲学（PPE）专业，获法学学士学位。本科期间师从冯志轩教授，曾主持珞珈本科生创新研究基金项目“全劳动生产率分层测算体系研究”。He graduated from the Hongyi Honor College at Wuhan University, where he majored in Politics, Economics, and Philosophy (PPE) and received a Bachelor of Laws degree. As an undergraduate, he was supervised by Professor Zhixuan Feng and led a Luojia Undergraduate Innovation Research Fund project entitled “Research on a Hierarchical Measurement System for Total Labor Productivity.”

Research interests

研究方向Research interests

  • 科技创新体系的政治经济学分析Political Economy of Innovation Systems
  • ABM、动力系统与统计均衡等计算方法在政治经济学中的应用Applications of computational methods—including agent-based modeling (ABM), dynamical systems, and statistical equilibrium—to political economy
